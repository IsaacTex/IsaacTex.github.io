## Treatment
*Your physical therapy services will be directed by one of our licensed physical therapists, not by a physical therapy aid. It is our policy to schedule you with the same therapist for each one of your visits. Occasionally, substitutions must be made due to staff vacation, illness, or participation in continuing education courses.*

*A copy of your initial evaluation will be sent to your referring physician, along with regular progress notes to keep your physician updated on your current functional status. Communication with all parties is a crucial component of your treatment program.*

*Upon discharge, you will be adequately prepared to leave physical therapy, and your home exercise program will be updated accordingly. You may contact your physical therapist anytime with questions or concerns.*

## What we offer
*Although we utilize weights and special resistance exercise equipment, we strongly advocate for minimal equipment use so that a client is able to go home and do exercises on their own without a big investment of space and money in equipment. Most functional performance is based on accurate and effective control of our bodies in a functional or sporting task, exercise machines are effective for isolating specific muscles for development but are limited in helping us to learn how to use muscle groups in functional and coordinated ways when performing whole body sporting activities or work related tasks.*
