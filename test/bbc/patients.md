## What to Expect
Expect to be at the office from 60 to 90 minutes on your first visit. You will undergo a comprehensive evaluation so we can establish an appropriate treatment plan.

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your appointment to discuss insurance and billing procedures with our front office staff and complete your new patient paperwork.

Appointments are made with the front office staff. You should check in and out with them at each visit.

## What to bring
1. Insurance cards
2. Identification
3. Physicians orders
4. Comfortable clothes for evaluation of body area being treated.
5. Printed and Completed [Forms](https://www.bodyandbalancecenter.com/forms)

*We will need to make a photocopy of your insurance to bill your insurance company. Our staff will gladly help you with questions regarding your insurance benefits or other payment options.*

## Cancellation Policy
*In order to accommodate each patient's schedule, reserving time slots for treatment is very important to operate our facility efficiently. Canceling an appointment at the last minute or failing to come in for a session limits our ability to meet the scheduling needs of our other patients and therapists. Therefore, we request that all cancellations be made at least 24 hours in advance. A $60.00 cancellation fee will be charged for cancellations with less than 24-hour notice.*
